Recipe

Wrap an ordinary ingredient list in a <recipe> tag. A web component detects the units, converts everything to metric, and rescales the whole recipe to however many servings you want.

Unit detection

Cups, ounces, pounds, teaspoons, tablespoons, pints, quarts, gallons, fl oz, grams…

Metric conversion

Volumes become ml / l, weights become g / kg — rounded to something a cook can actually measure.

Serving scaler

Change the servings and every amount recalculates instantly. Counts keep nice fractions (½, ⅓, ¾).

Try it: change the Servings value, or use − / +. Amounts turn amber while rescaled. Hover an amount to see the original.
