Buttermilk Pancakes
Ingredients
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 cups buttermilk
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 oz butter, melted
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • salt to taste

Method

  1. Sift the flour into a bowl and whisk in one egg until you have a smooth paste.
  2. Pour in half a cup of the buttermilk, then beat in the remaining two eggs.
  3. Melt a quarter of a cup of butter and fold it through with the two onions.
  4. Rest the batter, then bake for 20 minutes at 350 degrees in a 9 inch pan.
